Rescue Radar — Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

Rescue Radar ("the app," "we," "us") helps people track migraine/headache rescue medication use. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address, and if you sign in with Google or Apple, basic profile info those services share with us (typically name and email) — used only to authenticate you, never for advertising.
  • Health and tracking information you enter: medications and doses you log, the date/time of entries, trigger tags you select, free-text notes you write, and any life/medication-change events you record. This is the core purpose of the app and is entered entirely by you.
  • Profile information: names you give to profiles (e.g. your own name, or someone else's if you track on their behalf), and, if you invite someone to view or help manage a profile, their email address for that invitation.
  • Notification preferences: whether you've enabled a daily logging reminder, and what time. Reminders are scheduled entirely on your device as local notifications — the reminder itself never leaves your phone or touches our servers.

How your information is used

  • To provide the app's core functionality: storing and displaying your medication logs, computing rebound-risk indicators, and rendering your trends.
  • To operate account sign-in and, if you use it, profile sharing with people you explicitly invite.
  • We do not sell your information, and we do not use your health data for advertising or share it with data brokers.

Where your information is stored

Data is stored in a managed cloud database (Supabase, built on PostgreSQL), secured with row-level access controls so that only you — and anyone you've explicitly invited to a given profile — can read or write that profile's data.

Sharing a profile with others

If you choose to share a profile (e.g. with a family member helping manage someone's migraines), the person you invite can see the data at the access level you grant them (view, log, or manage). You can revoke that access at any time from Settings.

Your choices

  • You can edit or delete any log entry, event, or note at any time within the app.
  • You can revoke a collaborator's access to a shared profile at any time.
  • You can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at the email below.

Children

Rescue Radar is intended for a general adult audience and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Not medical advice

Rescue Radar is a personal tracking tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and nothing in the app should be treated as a substitute for guidance from a qualified healthcare provider.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "last updated" date above.
