Rescue Radar — Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: [DATE]

You're in control of your data in Rescue Radar. There are two levels of deletion, depending on what you want removed:

Delete a single profile's tracking data

If you track for multiple people (or just want to wipe one profile's history), you can do this yourself, instantly, in the app:

  1. Open Rescue Radar and switch to the profile you want to delete.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Scroll to the delete-profile option and confirm.

This permanently removes that profile's medications, log entries, and events. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Delete your entire account

To delete your account (login, all profiles, and all associated data) entirely:

  1. Email nick.altebrando@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account, with the subject "Delete my account."
  2. We'll confirm the request and permanently delete your account and all associated data within 30 days.
  3. You'll receive a confirmation email once deletion is complete.
